Anthropic released Opus 5 today, only about a month after releasing Sonnet 5, and Fable/Mythos. We’re approaching ludicrous speed for software delivery timelines.

In the software world we used to expect annual releases for new versions of software. Even in the AI space releases would be 3-6 months between versions. And companies would often take turns, responding with new versions as competition.

Now in the last month we’ve had Fable/Mythos, Sonnet 5, GPT 5.6 (Sol/Luna/Terra), Kimi, and now Opus 5 reported to be Anthropic’s best coding model.

From an article at The Verge:
“The company said in a release that Opus 5 ‘comes close to the capabilities of Claude Fable 5 in many domains’ and is much better at complex coding tasks.”

If we keep increasing at this speed, we’re going to end up going plaid.
