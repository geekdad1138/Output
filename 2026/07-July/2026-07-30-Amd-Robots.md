AMD just announced their Robotics Partner Network, and their Robotics Software Suite to try and move heavily into the physical AI space.

At their Advancing AI 2026 conference, they announced products like Kria Robotics Stack (KRS), Robotics Core SDK, Physical AI SDK, Ryzen AI, ROCm, and HIP.

These products are directly aimed at their biggest competitor NVIDIA’s products like Omniverse, Isaac Sim, and CUDA.

From FIERCE Sensors:
“AMD announced a comprehensive approach to advancing robotics and other physical AI on Thursday that combines System-on-Chip silicon with open software designed to rival Nvidia’s CUDA along with an ecosystem of partners including nine robot makers.”

AMD’s approach is to make their systems open, integrating with systems like Gazebo or ROS 2, and offering their own open systems like ROCm and HIP.

AMD’s statement was clear:
“No single company will build the future of physical AI, AMD officials said. With the open concept, AMD ‘wants to remove vendor lock-in,’ Bhaaskar said. ‘Open source helps innovation.’ “
