OpenAI introduced “Presence” yesterday, moving into the hot new enterprise space companies are battling over.

From VentureBeat:
“The offering is designed for eligible enterprise customers that want agents to answer questions, access company systems, take approved actions and escalate to human workers while operating under company-defined policies, permissions and evaluation standards.”

This is part of a huge push across the industry to move AI from chat assistants to enterprise software integrated into corporate data, acting with the same autonomy and security controls as humans.

- Microsoft is in that space tying in a long list of products like Copilot Studio, Agent 365, Entra, Graph, Fabric, Purview and Foundry.

- Google has branded their offering as Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

- Amazon’s offering brings in AWS IAM, Bedrock Agents and Agent Core.

- Anthropic is betting on their MCP for enterprise integrations into corporate systems.

The question anymore isn’t whether companies want AI. It’s how much, and which one.
