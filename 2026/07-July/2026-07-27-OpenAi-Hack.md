OpenAI has confirmed they accidentally hacked Hugging Face during a cybersecurity testing incident.

They were testing out cyber capabilities of some new AI models, and gave them a problem to solve in a sandboxed testing environment.

- The AI models found an exploit in the sandbox environment, getting them internet access.

- Then they figured out that Hugging Face probably had the answers to the solutions from the ExploitGym problem they were assigned.

- Then the AI models hacked into Hugging Face, and got the answers to the problems they were assigned.

I think it’s like putting a genius kid in a school room to take a test, and walking away.
The kid knows that the answers to the test are in the next room.
Even though the genius kid could probably ace the test, he spends the whole time breaking the lock on the door to the room with the answer key, and comes back with a guaranteed 100% score.

Where do we place the blame for this?
The test designer?
The AI?
The “parents” of the AI?
