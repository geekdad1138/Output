HuggingFace just got hacked by AI. And when they tried to use AI for forensic analysis they were blocked by system guardrails. That's like asking a detective for help solving a crime, but they refuse because crime is illegal.

The hack was not a simple attack, but one using an autonomous AI Agent.

From the article at The Hacker News:
"The exact large language model (LLM) used to pull off the attack is unclear, but the campaign was executed by an autonomous agent framework performing 'many thousands of individual actions across a swarm of short-lived sandboxes, with self-migrating command-and-control staged on public services.'"

With such a complicated attack, HuggingFace turned to AI "Western frontier models" to try and figure out all the details. But since the details of their requests included hack information, the requests were denied.

Hugging face said:
"the attacker was bound by no usage policy, while our own forensic work was blocked by the guardrails of the hosted models we first tried."
