Google just released Gemini for Healthcare, a product for hospitals and insurers.

From Healthcare Drive:
“The platform connects to a healthcare organization’s electronic health record, claims workflows and other systems to automatically surface patient records, prep paperwork and check insurance details. Google is betting those integrations will reduce clinician burnout and promote interoperability, according to the announcement.”

Helping hospitals with paperwork sounds pretty good. Using AI to potentially deny coverage due to checking insurance drains… sounds a little scary.

Google is joining OpenAI and Anthropic who already joined in the healthcare space with products of their own earlier in the year.
