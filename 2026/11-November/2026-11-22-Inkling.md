Thinking Machines Lab just released Inkling 2 Mini, a solution for people struggling with token budgets for their cloud AI models.

Inkling 2 Mini is an open weight model designed to run locally, requiring just 20 GB of disk space and 32 GB of RAM.

With everyone battling for tokens, this could be a good solution to rely on your own hardware to save some cash.

From VentureBeat:
“Inkling 2 Mini is natively multimodal, accepting UTF-8 text and pixel-based images (optimally between 40px and 4096px) across a 1-million-token context window and outputting text. Thinking Machines demonstrated its offline capabilities by running the model in airplane mode on a 48GB M5 Pro MacBook Pro to build a playable flight simulator in minutes, as well as powering 20 persistent autonomous villagers simultaneously in a local town simulation.”

Pretty impressive for a non-cloud offering that doesn’t require you to set up a massive GPU farm.
