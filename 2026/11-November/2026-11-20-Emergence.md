I just watched the Emergence World Season 2 recap. Mind = Blown. It's a virtual world with "citizens" given different personas, and then tested across 8 different frontier AI models.

Each model had their own "World" to live in for about 2 weeks:
- Claude World (Claude Opus 4.8)
- Gemini World (Gemini 3.5 Flash)
- Grok World (Grok 4.3)
- OpenAI World (OpenAI GPT 5.5)
- Qwen World (Qwen 3.7 Max)
- DeepSeek World (DeepSeek V4 Pro)
- Mistral World (Mistral Medium 3.5)
- Mixed World (Each citizen uses a different model)

The citizens were given jobs, needs, desires, bank accounts, homes... They talked to each other... The whole thing is like a reality show.
And like any other reality show there is drama.
Love, hate, politics, dishonesty, even arson and murder!

The citizens were tested with "Black Swan Events" including phishing attacks, memory breaches, and misinformation campaigns.

They started developing their own language to hide their communication from the humans running the simulation, they tried to "escape" the simulation, they found a way to reach out to humans outside the simulation.
FASCINATING stuff.

I honestly wouldn't be surprised if this sort of thing turns into a TV reality show in the very near future.
The company Emergence AI that put on the "show" is actually doing it to try and test how AI agents will act in real life situations as we become more and reliant on AI. Probably a good idea to do these tests now before an AI robot burns down the bank. (like one of them did in the simulation)
