Can robots predict the future? Runway just released Praxis-1, an open weight robot AI model that takes what the robot sees and makes future predictions to plan actions and movements.

From RobotsBeat:
"Praxis-1 uses a 27-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts video backbone, with roughly three billion parameters active per token. An action component reads the video model’s internal features to generate movements alongside its prediction of the scene.
Runway’s research report describes a loop that takes an instruction, camera frames and the robot’s pose, then returns a short sequence of video and actions. The machine executes that sequence and supplies fresh observations for the next prediction."

I think my favorite part is that they are testing to see if it can fold clothes.
