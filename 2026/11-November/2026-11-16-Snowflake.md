If you missed it last week, Snowflake's Expedition featured more than 40 sessions, split across tracks for builders and leaders. 

I didn't have time to watch every session but I enjoyed the ones I saw.

I think my favorite was:
"State of Databases: A Fresh Foundation for Agents and Apps" by Craig Kerstiens and Allison Lee

Over and over again I see reports talking about how having clean structured data is important for AI, and this presentation really got into the history of data and where we are today.
