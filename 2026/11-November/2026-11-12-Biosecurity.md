The UK is spending £100m on the worlds first AI Biosecurity Lab, to use in the fight against biological threats.

From the BBC:
“But the AI Minister Kanishka Narayan MP said it would be a "major new global institution" that brings together "world-leading scientific expertise" to ensure the UK can safeguard its citizens and goals as AI develops.”

Both OpenAI and Anthropic are providing £200m in token usage toward the project.
