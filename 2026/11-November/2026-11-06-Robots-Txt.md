Global rules for AI robots? No this isn’t Asimov’s laws. This is for the robots.txt file that is used to tell web crawlers what not to crawl.

In the past websites had to build out specific rules for every single company’s AI accessing their content for training.

Now Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic have all agreed to follow a single simple “keep out” rule.

From TNW:
“The shared signal comes from an IETF draft by Kevin Kelley of Anthropic, published on Friday. The same day, Google, OpenAI and Microsoft announced they would support it. Kelley announced the draft on the IETF’s AI preferences mailing list.”
