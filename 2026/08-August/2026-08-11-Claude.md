Claude is now hiding secret messages in your code as a watermark to identify AI generated code.

As part of their requirement to comply with the EU AI Act in effect this week, Anthropic released that they are watermarking not only images, but also carving their initials into the code they generate.

From explainx.ai:
“Claude models launched on or after August 2, 2026 weave an imperceptible watermark directly into generated text”

The fancy word for what they’re doing is “steganography” which sadly has nothing to do with dinosaurs, and wholly to do with math magic.

Anthropic has claimed that it won’t affect the code at all.
