Is it time for the indium phosphide (InP) boom? NVIDIA has already committed billions to the top InP suppliers, and Lumentum is warning of massive shortages.

From TrendForce:
“Lumentum CEO Michael Hurlston has warned that the supply-demand gap for indium phosphide (InP) has now surpassed that of DRAM and NAND, making it one of the most critical constraints on AI data center optical interconnect expansion.”

InP is a huge part of data centers, because of its capabilities in optical communications.

China is currently responsible for 70% of indium production, which involves mining for zinc and collecting it as a byproduct. As well as the majority of the InP production facilities.
