When the NSA, CISA, FBI, DOE, and EPA all get together to warn everyone about PLCs getting hacked… we might be in trouble.

On Wednesday the government agencies released a cybersecurity advisory warning of critical security vulnerabilities for PLCs.

From The Hacker News:
“Targets of the activity include Critical Manufacturing, Energy, Water and Wastewater Systems, Chemical, Food and Agriculture, and Commercial Facilities.“

All of those are pretty important.
And PLCs these days aren’t like the closed locked simple machines running basic ladder logic like they used to be.
They have WiFi, TCP/IP, web servers, some can even load Linux or Windows.

The AI attacks are apparently mostly using Python, and disguising themselves as monitoring software.
