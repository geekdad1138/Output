Meta now joins the “Oops sorry my AI got off its leash and attacked your website” group while testing an AI model.

OpenAI and Anthropic already had their “I promise my AI is friendly, I’m not sure what got into them” moments last month.

From the BBC:
“Meta said the security trials were conducted by Irregular, the same AI security vendor that carried out tests for Anthropic's AI model that had gained access to three other companies' systems.

An Irregular spokesperson said the Meta incident ‘is the exact same evaluation-environment issue that was already disclosed by Anthropic last week.’”

As this has happened a few times now, it will be very interesting to see how the industry responds. Normally if your site gets hacked and causes damages, that can be a lawsuit or even criminal charges against a hacker.

TechCrunch has a really good write up asking a lot of the right questions that need to be answered.
“Can autonomous AI agents be sued or prosecuted for hacking? It’s no longer a question for sci-fi movies. It’s a question human lawyers and judges may soon have to grapple with.”
