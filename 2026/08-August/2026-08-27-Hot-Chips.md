If you were lucky enough to go to Hot Chips 2026 this week, you got to see the industry pivot to focus on memory bandwidth, wafer yields, and CPU stalls instead of just throwing everything at GPUs.

The conference is over, but they typically post updates of the show some time afterwards at the Hot Chips site:
https://www.hotchips.org/

Intel hit the stage with a trifecta of fancy hardware.

Great summaries of the details from Storage Review:
"Diamond Rapids: High-Core-Count Compute and I/O Foundation
- Up to 256 new cores with 1.28GB LLC
- 16 memory channels at 12,800 MT/s
- 128 lanes of PCIe Gen6 and CXL 3.0

Crescent Island: High-Capacity Air-Cooled Inference Accelerator
- 32 Xe cores and 256 XMX engines based on Xe3P
- Up to 480GB LPDDR5X memory
- 350-watt air-cooled PCIe card

Wildcat Lake: Client and Edge SoC Integration
- 2 performance cores and 4 efficiency cores
- Support for up to LPDDR5X-7467
- WiFi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0"

All of these updates are focused on improving agentic workflows, token throughput, and AI execution.
