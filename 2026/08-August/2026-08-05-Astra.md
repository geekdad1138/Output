OpenAI’s new model Astra is apparently a math super genius. In one day, it solved ten math problems that stumped humans for decades.

I’m not going to pretend to understand the math problems, but apparently it solved them and verified them with Lean. Originally launched in 2013, Lean is a functional programming language that can be used to demonstrate and test mathematical proofs.

There hasn’t been an official release for Astra yet, or even whether it will be GPT 5.7 or GPT 6.

Apparently they opened up the model, pointed it at some super tough math problems, and it went Good Will Hunting on them in just a day.
