The microduck revolution is upon us. Hugging Face has launched a new duck robot toy for $400 that you can train and play with.

From TechCrunch:
“Clem Delangue 🤗, CEO of Hugging Face, said the Microduck is an “open-source robot you can teach new tricks with reinforcement learning.” The 25-centimeter-tall duck can waddle, pick things up with its beak, get back up when it falls, crouch, and even roller skate.”

This development came about as part of their acquisition of Pollen Robotics last year.

Microduck has a camera and lidar, and can be programmed using the Hugging Face software.
You train the duck in the simulation software, export it to ONNX format (Open Neural Network Exchange) then load it on the duck.
