DEF CON 34 had a robot hackathon last weekend testing people’s ability to break into physical AI, ROS2, and robotic platforms.

From the Robotic Hacking Community (RHC) group at the DEF CON site:
“Hackers have broken browsers. 
Hackers have broken cars. 
Now we're hacking robots.”

The goal of the group was to test the security and safety of these AI enabled systems, as vulnerabilities can have real world consequences.

The community was organized by VicOne, and after DEF CON was over they released free software for testing attacks.

From VicOne:
“VicOne, a Physical AI cybersecurity solutions leader, today released the free VicOne Radeis Extension for NVIDIA Isaac Sim™, enabling robotics developers to test how selected cyber-safety attack scenarios could affect the behavior of their own robot models in simulation.”
