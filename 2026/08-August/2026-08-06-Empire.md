New York’s Empire AI project “AI research for the public good” has now launched the Beta phase of its project.

The state project is a supercomputing cluster, offering powerful capabilities to academic groups for research.

The Alpha phase launched with 144 HGX H100 GPUs, and 48 HGX H200 GPUs.

The Beta phase has added another 288 DGX B200 GPUs.

Not quite as many GPUs as the hundreds of thousands in use by the massive tech companies, but the intent is to give academic researchers access to these resources without relying on corporations and paying big fees.

And the cool part? The cooling for this supercomputing center is part of University at Buffalo’s Clean Energy Master Plan (CEMP). They are running huge water pipes to take all that heat generated and use it to heat campus buildings.
