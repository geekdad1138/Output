Anthropic just announced their new Model Hardware Standard (MHS) to help AIs integrate easily with hardware. Similar to how they built MCP to help AI easily integrate with software.

From Techpresso:
"Anthropic on August 27 opened a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a shared specification that lets AI agents operate physical laboratory and manufacturing instruments, microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms, plate readers, in parallel. Read it as a preview, not a general release: MHS only works with devices that expose a programmable interface, and a first group of scientific research labs and advanced manufacturers are applying for access, not downloading a public SDK."

MHS still requires a fair bit of work to set up, as devices will need an MHS driver for LLMs to be able to communicate with them using MHS. But if you have 10 unique devices in your environment and they all have MHS drivers, now the LLM can speak to all of them and coordinate actions between them.

Anthropic is only allowing a small set of companies access right now, and you have to apply for access at: https://www.modelhardwarestandard.com/
