AMD just acquired Taalas, a company that specializes in etching AI models directly into hardware, instead of using software to load a model.

From the Taalas website:
“Taalas is creating a platform for quickly turning any AI model into custom silicon. The resulting Hardcore Models are 1000x more efficient than their software counterparts.”

So Taalas could create a “hardcore” version of Kimi K3 built directly into hardware, running orders of magnitude faster than using a traditional GPU.

Being acquired by AMD means Taalas gets massive exposure to the computing market, and AMD gets a new technology to help them in the AI tech speed race.

From CNBC:
“AMD said it would integrate the Taalas chips and technology into its road map, including in systems with its central processors and Instinct GPUs.”

Etching the model directly onto a chip means it’s locked and not flexible like other GPU setups, but if the trade off is running 1000x more efficiently then it might be worth the bet.
