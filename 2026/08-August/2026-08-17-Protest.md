StopAI protester Wynd Kaufmyn has been sentenced to a week in jail after chaining locks on the doors to OpenAI and refusing to move.

News reports are saying she is the first anti-AI protester to be jailed.

From the SF Standard:
“Kaufmyn is a member of StopAI, a protest group dedicated to halting the development of artificial superintelligence. Its members regularly participate in acts of civil disobedience, such as chaining themselves to the doors of AI companies.”

The StopAI organization is taking a hard stance against any future development of AI.
One of the principles from their website:
“- Pursue a permanent global ban on the further development of frontier AI technology.”

With companies in the AI space being valued at hundreds of billions of dollars, or even in some cases estimated at over a trillion, the race to make AI even more powerful doesn’t seem to be slowing down even if the doors are chained shut.
