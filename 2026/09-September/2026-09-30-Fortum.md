Google goes nuclear with its AI, partnering up with Fortum, a Finnish energy company to buy power from a nuclear power plant.

Because of their AI usage, Google has reported an increase in their carbon footprint. They are targeting to be net zero by 2030, so this agreement will be a big step for them to use carbon free energy.

From ESG Dive:
“Google is planning to invest over $15 billion (13 billion euros) in digital infrastructure to power its data center developments in Finland and Europe more broadly, the global tech conglomerate said Wednesday. The funding represents “the company’s largest single investment in Europe,” Ruth Porat of Alphabet Inc., Google’s parent company, said in the press release.”

They’re also working on nuclear power plants in Tennessee and Iowa.
