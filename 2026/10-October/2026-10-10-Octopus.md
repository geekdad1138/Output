Octopus Deploy just dropped their 2026 Future of Platform Engineering Report, and it's fantastic. The term Platform Engineering itself is still relatively new, and this report does a great job of explaining where we are at.

If you're not too familiar with the term, the report lists out DORA's definition of it as:
"Platform Engineering is a sociotechnical discipline that sits at the intersection of how teams work together and the technical work of automation, self-service, and repeatability"

CI/CD, IAC, code scanning, automated testing... all of those fancy things that help coders keep coding.

The 2026 report also dives into AI adoption and how that is affecting Platform Engineering.
It's got clear charts, diagrams and metrics listed out from the surveys.

One of the callout findings from their 2025 report was:
"Nearly 25% of organizations rely on subjective assessments rather than formal metrics to evaluate platform success. Those using metrics employ 1 to 8 different measures"

It's great to see reports like the ones Octopus delivers that focus on facts not feelings.

Check out the 2026 report here:
https://octopus.com/publications/future-of-platform-engineering-report

Authors: Charlotte Fleming, Steve Fenton, Heidi Waterhouse, Matt Allford, John Bristowe, Liam Mackie, Tim Nicholas, Denis Jajčević, Tod Thomson
