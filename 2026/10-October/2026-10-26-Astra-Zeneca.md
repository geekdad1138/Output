Last week AstraZeneca announced a $1.46 billion deal with the AI company insitro for their Virtual Human engine. The pharma giant is looking for AI help with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) and cardiomyopathies.

From Fierce Biotech:
“MASH and cardiomyopathies are prevalent, underdiagnosed diseases where high-content data holds immense signal for uncovering disease drivers,” insitro CEO insitro said in a statement. “By applying AI to these complex datasets, we can unlock greater discovery power and reveal novel genetic insights.”

Big pharma is betting big money on AI making big discoveries. AstraZeneca, Gilead, Bristol Myers Squibb, and Eli Lilly are all hoping to cash in on the new tech.
