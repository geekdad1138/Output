OpenAI joins the crowd of always on agents, announcing “dots” at their Dev Day event.

Almost a year ago OpenClaw hit the scene giving people the ability to plug always on agents into their daily lives.

Now we have Meta Muse, Grok Bot, Gemini Spark, Claude Cowork, OpenAI dots, and Microsoft Scout.

From alphamatch.ai:
“Self-hosted OpenClaw made always-on messaging agents mainstream earlier in 2026. Meta Muse turned the same idea into a consumer hit. xAI's Grok Bot put a persistent cloud computer behind named bots on Cursor plans. Dots is OpenAI's managed answer”

They all come in under different pricing plans and capabilities, and they’re all competing to be the one agent you choose to integrate in your life.
