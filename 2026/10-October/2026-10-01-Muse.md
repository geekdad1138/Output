Meta has now launched their Muse app on both iOS and Android. Their app offers full integration into applications like WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, Google Workspace, and Outlook. For a price.

The starting price for Muse is $20 a month for a daily user, or $100 a month for an enterprise level user.

From TechCrunch:
“The company says the agent can do things like sending emails, booking travel, lowering bills, filling out forms, creating plans, turning recipe reels into grocery lists, sending party invitations, and making purchases, leveraging Link by Stripe for checkout.”

This is a huge step for users to start trusting AI to integrate into their lives. Some people have already accepted this with programs like OpenClaw, but now Muse is an app, accessible via mobile to users.
