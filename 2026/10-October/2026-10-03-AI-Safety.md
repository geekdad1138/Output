In the global policing of AI, who can you trust to follow the rules? The Future of Life institute (FLI) is a group that helps to study and report on focus areas like artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and nuclear weapons.

They keep a scorecard ranking for the big AI companies in the world, which can be found here: (spoiler, no one ranked above a C+)
https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/#scorecard

Multiple AI companies have called for a slow down. Anthropic’s Dario Amodei made a statement saying “We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models,”

OpenAI’s Sam Altman commented as well saying “We welcome a federal framework that sets consistent safety requirements for frontier AI”

It’s good to see the top American companies voicing their support for caution with innovation.

But the AI race is global and not limited to just American companies. And even with groups like FLI publishing reports, they may not have access to private developments.

It’s all fun and great to get all these cool new AI tools.

But if someone doesn’t make sure we don’t accidentally make the big scary world killing AI we all saw in the sci-fi movies.. we’re going to have a bad time real soon.
