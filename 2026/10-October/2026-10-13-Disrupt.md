TechCrunch’s Disrupt 2026 is wrapped up! If you were lucky enough to attend in San Francisco they had an amazing line up of presenters talking about everything from AI, to robotics, climate tech and finance.

They had different stages, each with their own catagory: Disrupt, Builders, AI, Smart Money, Smart Systems, Real World

From TechCrunch:
“Day 1 of TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 is officially underway, bringing together 10,000+ startup founders, investors, and tech leaders under one roof at San Francisco’s Moscone West. Big ideas are colliding, connections are forming at every turn, and the startup ecosystem’s most influential voices are driving the conversation.”
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/techcrunch-disrupt-2026-day-1/

They had over 250 speakers at this event spanning three days across multiple industries. A lot of the usual companies you would expect like Google, NVIDIA, OpenAI but also a lot of others that are start ups or rising stars. From AI to robotics to.. wait.. Mark Wahlberg? Yes that Mark Wahlberg.
