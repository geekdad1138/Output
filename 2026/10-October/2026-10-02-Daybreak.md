OpenAI is teaming up with MS-ISAC to use their  Daybreak initiative to protect critical systems like water, electricity and banking.

MS-ISAC is the Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center. They work across all 50 states to help with cybersecurity tasks and information.

From the Center for Internet Security:
“The MS-ISAC is the nation’s trusted cybersecurity partner for U.S. State, Local, Tribal, and Territorial (SLTT) government organizations. We work across key sectors including law enforcement and public safety, education, healthcare, and critical infrastructure to help our members strengthen their cyber resilience and reduce risk.”

OpenAI is teaming up with MS-ISAC and other key groups to offer up free or heavily discounted use of their frontier models to help protect critical systems. They call it their Daybreak for Frontline Defenders initiative, and they are offering up one billion dollars of compute to help.

From Industrial Cyber:
“Daybreak for America brings together all of OpenAI’s U.S. work to protect the systems Americans rely on every day, from water and electricity to local government and banking, including a new pilot with the Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center (MS-ISAC).”
