Just finished listening to the AI engineering audio book by Chip Huyen, narrated by Edelyn Okano. In a word? Comprehensive. This book is not the type of book that you just listen to once and put on a shelf and think about.

This is the type of book you keep on your desk and you use as a reference for everything in AI engineering from prompt engineering to finetuning to inference optimization.

Edelyn did a great job narrating, very clear and concise. I listen to a lot of audiobooks, especially about technical subjects. And it’s very important to have someone like this who speaks clearly and brings the words from the page to life.

If anyone is seeking a career in AI engineering, I highly recommend you go pick up this book. Read it or listen to it cover to cover. And then keep it around for review.
