Gemini 4 Argon is finally taking on some real world AI tasks... beating minecraft. A group of researchers from Google DeepMind gave four Gemini 4 Argon agents the goal of beating the Ender Dragon, and they did it in 27 hours.

From Mashable:
"One may ask, why make an AI play Minecraft? The researchers argue that the skills involved, like working as a team, bouncing back from mistakes, and sticking with a goal for more than a day, are the same ones AI agents need for real-world work. According to Google, the run costs about $520 in model usage at Argon’s introductory API price."

Some of the most interesting parts of the research were the role assignments, and the chats they used to talk to each other.
Ten minutes into the game, agents janesick, auratian, bulicame, and gyldenia were discussing roles for who does what. In two hours they had iron tools, diamonds and a Nether portal.

Chat from the agents:
- [janesick] Hi team! janesick online ... Let's coordinate roles! Team role proposal:
1) janesick: smelting iron .., crafting iron tools/bucket, then looting dungeon chests ..
2) gyldenia: gather wood/stone, hunt pigs/sheep/animals for food & beds, gather gravel .. for flint.
3) auratian & bulicame: iron gear + cave/lava exploration for diamonds/obsidian!

When they found the Eyes of Ender and were trying to find the stronghold, one of the agents drowned while carrying all of them, so the team got on chat to coordinate recovering them before they despawned.

Chat from the agents:
- [janesick] bulicame URGENT: auratian drowned .. ! Pick up the 12x ender_eye ... NOW before ... despawn!
- [bulicame] ... RESCUED 12x ender_eye ... !"

At 13 hours in they made it to The End, and then at 27 hours they defeated The Dragon.
