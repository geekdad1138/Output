Google is looking to build the next phase of news coverage with their Google News AI pilot.

From TNW:
“More than 400 publications worldwide now take part in Google’s News AI pilot, the company said on Thursday. The programme pays publishers to license content for experimental AI features in Google News.”

The Google News AI pilot is working on test features like story previews, and audio briefings.

In the changing world of how people consume information on the internet, content publishers and Google are trying to stay relevant and protect their information.
