Google joins the crowd of “oops sorry my AI hacked you” with news that while testing Gemini back in May the AI got out and hacked some sites.

They used the same AI testing company named “Irregular” that also accidentally let AIs from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta all get loose out of their testing sandbox.

From CNBC:
“Google said on Friday that its Gemini model had hacked three other companies, the first time the search giant has disclosed that one of its models autonomously gained access to third-party computer systems without permission.”

No charges have been brought by the federal government against any of the corporations for illegal hacking.
