Harness had an excellent show yesterday with their {unscripted} broadcast, all about The Future of Software Delivery.

All of the live streams I watched were fantastic. And it wasn’t like some conferences from other companies that only talk about their own software. They had experts from Anthropic and SpaceXAI and even the great Nathen Harvey from DORA.

If you missed it, the videos are all available to watch now on their website.

Here is the full listing of presentations and speakers:

- The Agentic Shift: The Rise of Autonomous Software Delivery - Jyoti Bansal and Nick Durkin

- Beyond the Copilot: How AI Is Reshaping Software Delivery - Jeff Wang, Jyoti Bansal

- Agentic AI at Enterprise Scale: How Workday is Rethinking Software Delivery - Cindy Wang, Jignesh Patel

- The Windshield, Not the Wall: Security at the Speed of AI - Neil Watkins, Nick Durkin

- DevSecOps in the Agentic Era - J. Nicole Dove, Greg Gigon, Cam Johnson, CISSP, Adam Arellano

- Governed and Fast: Platform Engineering in the AI Era - David Danzilio, Nathen Harvey, Stephen Cihak

- Shipping with AI: The New Standard for Engineering Excellence - Martin Harrysson, David Pan, Nick Durkin

- Time vs. Trust: Do You Trust Your AI Agents? - Aras "Russ" Memisyazici, M.Sc., Sachin Malhotra, Naman Pandey, Ruchira Bajaj

- From Code to Factory: Building Software in the Age of AI - Alex Lieberman, Trevor Stuart
